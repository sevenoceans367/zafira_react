## Independent continuation findings (2026-11-26)

### Visual and summary behavior

The summary is a fixed-width floating card layered over the page. It does not expand the full summary bar. At 1536×960 bottom scroll with the panel expanded, the panel wrapper ends at y=855 and Save/Close occupy y=882–919 (27px gap). At 1024×768 with sidebar expanded, the wrapper ends at y=610 and actions occupy y=697–734. At 1920×1080 the collapsed pill is at y=951–995 and actions at y=1023–1060. Summary open at top, middle, and bottom did not obscure footer actions. The muted gray SOF-style footer is present, as requested; the supplied HTML prototype itself has a yellow/orange notice.

The page document has no horizontal overflow at tested widths. The table wrappers do overflow and are horizontally scrollable, especially with the sidebar expanded at 1280px and 1024px. This is a responsive usability limitation, not whole-page overflow. T3 snapshots are viewport-sized; no full-page shots could be captured.

These are rendered GET-backed page values; isolated-clone SQL and Legacy runtime parity remain unverified. LP-Singapore also initially rendered NOR Tendered `31-02-2026 08:00` with a red border but no `aria-invalid` attribute.

On an unsaved blank DP-Advario call, a 10h C100 activity with 72h allowance, USD 24,000/day demurrage, and USD 12,000/day dispatch rendered 10.0000 actual hours and USD 31,000 dispatch. Setting LT Partial to 150 made the field `aria-invalid=true`, marked calculations unavailable, and replaced financial summary values with dashes. Save remained enabled; it was not clicked because the API resolves to shared port 3306. Thus Save rejection and SQL persistence are not proven.
